# Fact check, October 2026

Scope: all 50 cards, 106 unique source links, English as the master text. Every change was copied into all 9 languages using the same number and unit rules as the rest of the site. No verdict was changed.

## How it was checked
- Every source link was opened by a script. Links that refused bots (403 or a consent wall) were opened again in a real headless Chrome.
- The key figures on each card were compared with the cited source, or with a current primary source when the cited one was vague.
- Limit: the opinion and argument parts of the cards were not "verified". They are arguments, not figures. A few news sites (Zeit, Süddeutsche, Volkskrant, NYT, tesla.com pages) show a consent wall or block automated browsers. They looked alive, but their article text could not be read automatically.

## Cards that need your judgment (verdicts NOT changed)
1. **elon-unfit / drugs ("Overstated")**: since 2025 the NYT and others reported heavy ketamine and other drug use. Musk denies it and no test or medical record is public. The card is still defensible, but the reporting has moved. Consider adding a sentence about it.
2. **tesla-autopilot-death**: in January 2026 Tesla dropped basic Autopilot on new US and Canadian cars. The card reads as if Autopilot is current. The verdict is fine, but you may want to add one line.
3. **tesla-fire**: Tesla's figure is global, while the NFPA figure covers the US only. The card says this, but the kernel still calls them "gasoline fires", and the NFPA count includes all highway vehicles. Consider "ordinary vehicle fires".
4. **elon-subsidies, elon-cofounder**: "2009 DOE loan" is fine (offered 2009, closed January 2010). Saying "2010" would be more exact.
5. **Sources that are opinion or advocacy** (Heritage, Cato, book pages) back cap-nordic, cap-exploit and spacex-stole-nasa. The figures check out, but a neutral data source next to them would make them harder to attack.

## Decisions (October 22, 2026)
- elon-unfit: removed (no solid source confirms or rules it out). Replaced by elon-degree ("no physics degree", False). /elon/unfit/ now redirects to /elon/degree/ in every language.
- tesla-fire: Tesla publishes only a worldwide figure, and no reliable worldwide all-vehicle fire rate exists. The card now says so and explains why the ratio carries over. Swedish MSB data (same agency for all cars) is added as a like-for-like check. "Gasoline fires" is now "vehicle fires (all fuel types)".
- cap-nordic, cap-exploit, spacex-stole-nasa: a visible note about think-tank sources was added.
- tesla-autopilot-death and the 2009 DOE loan wording: kept as is.
